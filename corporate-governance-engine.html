    Optimal State by Use Case

    Asset Protection / IP Holding

    ♦ Nevada
    Strongest charging order protection, no state income tax, high privacy, no nexus if passive income only.

    Active California Business

    🌊 California
    NV/NY entities must foreign-qualify if conducting business in CA — triggering $800 min franchise tax. Domestic CA entity avoids double-registration.

    Holding Company Structure

    ♦ Nevada
    NV holding + CA operating: IP/assets in Nevada holding receive royalty payments from CA operating company, shifting income to no-tax jurisdiction.

    East Coast Operations / Finance

    🗽 New York
    NY is essential for finance, media, and legal practices with NYC client base. Factor in publication costs ($1,300+ in NYC metro).

    S-Corp Tax Savings

    🌊 California
    Form S-corp in state of operations. NV/NY S-corps operating in CA still pay CA tax — no savings. True savings come from entity structure, not just state selection.

    Maximum Privacy

    ♦ Nevada
    NV LLCs do not disclose member names publicly. Registered agent on public record. No operating agreement required to be filed. Strongest overall privacy.
